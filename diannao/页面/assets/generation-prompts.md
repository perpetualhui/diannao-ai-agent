# Hand asset generation

Execution: built-in image_gen tool; transparent_background: true.
The tool does not expose a selectable model/version parameter.
Original generated alpha channels are preserved without background removal or clipping masks.

## Human hand

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: premium website hero transparent PNG, standalone left-side human hand and forearm for an interactive fingertip-touch animation. New original asset, not a cutout from a screenshot.
Primary request: Generate one anatomically correct adult human hand and a long bare forearm, reaching gracefully from LEFT toward RIGHT, index finger extended toward a robotic counterpart (the counterpart must NOT appear in this image).
Scene/backdrop: actual TRANSPARENT background with a real alpha channel. Nothing behind or beneath the arm; no visible checkerboard.
Composition/framing: landscape 1536 x 1024 canvas. Forearm enters the LEFT canvas edge at approximately y=690; wrist around x=650,y=560; index fingertip at approximately x=1320,y=420, pointing horizontally to the right. Keep the entire index finger and all remaining fingers inside the canvas. The arm extends seamlessly to and beyond the LEFT boundary, with no visible severed stump; only the forearm is cropped at that boundary. The fingertip is the rightmost occupied point, with about 12 percent empty transparent margin to its right. Fingers are gently bent except the reaching index finger. Natural, elegant gesture similar to two index fingers about to touch, with back and side of hand visible, not palm-facing camera. No sleeve, no jewelry.
Style/medium: high-end photorealistic studio photograph with subtle warm medium skin tone, extremely convincing skin pores, knuckle folds, small veins and fine soft arm hair. Tasteful restrained editorial art direction for a minimal light-gray futuristic homepage.
Lighting: broad soft daylight from upper left, neutral white light; realistic dimensional highlights and gentle self-shadowing, no hard rim light or colored light.
Constraints: EXACTLY ONE human hand, five anatomically correct fingers, believable joints and fingernails, long continuous forearm. NO robotic hand, second hand, head, torso, text, letters, logo, watermark, scenery, floor, backdrop gradient, glow, circle, shadow cast onto a background, or UI. Preserve true alpha transparency, clean naturally antialiased edges.
